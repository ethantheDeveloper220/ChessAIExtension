# Chess AI Extension ♟️🤖

Welcome to the **Chess AI Extension**! 🎉 This Chrome extension helps you **validate your chess moves** using an AI that provides **instant feedback**. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, this extension is here to help you improve your chess game! ♟️💡

---

## Features ✨

- 🧠 **AI-Powered Move Validation**: Get real-time feedback on your moves.
- 💻 **Chrome Extension**: Easy to install and use in your browser.
- ⚡ **Interactive Chessboard**: Make moves and instantly test them with the AI.
- 🏆 **Enhance Your Skills**: Learn from your mistakes and become a chess master.

---

## How to Install 🔧

1. **Download the Extension**:
   - Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/ethantheDeveloper220/ChessAIExtension/releases) page.
   - Download the `.zip` file for the latest version.

2. **Install the Extension**:
   - Open **Chrome** and go to `chrome://extensions/`.
   - Enable **Developer Mode** at the top right.
   - Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder of the extension.

3. **Start Playing**:
   - Once installed, open the extension and start making moves on the chessboard.
   - Use the **"Test Move"** button to get instant feedback from the AI! 🤖

---

## How to Use 🎮

1. **Make a Move**: Click on the chessboard to make your move.
2. **Click "Test Move"**: After making your move, click the **Test Move** button to validate your move with AI feedback.
3. **Improve**: Use the feedback to improve your gameplay and become a better chess player! 🏅
